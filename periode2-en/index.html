Welcome to Period 2: Prototype & AZ-900 Exam Prep

In Period 1 you researched, justified, and started learning Azure. In this period you'll put that knowledge to work by building a small working Azure prototype (Assignment 4), and then prepare for the real AZ-900: Microsoft Azure Fundamentals certification exam. From here on, everything is in English — just like the actual exam.

Where this fits in your exam portfolio

Assignment 4 is the final piece of kerntaak D1-K1: Oriënteren op en verdiepen in arbeidsmarktrelevante software (werkproces D1-K1-W2). Once you've completed it, along with the assignments from Period 1, you'll assemble your exam portfolio and prepare for your eindgesprek (final interview) with your examiner — see the resources page linked below.
