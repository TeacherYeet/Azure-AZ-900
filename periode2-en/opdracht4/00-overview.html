Assignment 4 · D1-K1-W1

Prototype

In this assignment you design and build a small, working prototype using Microsoft Azure. You keep the scope deliberately small — a static website or a basic web app is enough — but you build it for real: deployed on Azure, reachable through a live URL, and documented like a professional would document it.

💡 What you will build
A simple Azure architecture that actually works. For example: a static website hosted on an Azure Storage Account, or a basic web app running on Azure App Service (Free F1 tier) or Azure Static Web Apps (free tier). You use free-tier resources or your student/school credits, so this will not cost you anything.

What does this involve?

You go through four phases: you propose what you are going to build, you build it step by step while keeping a log, you document the architecture, cost and security of what you built, and you hand in your logbook together with the working result.

  1. Propose: describe the prototype you want to build — its functionality, its look, and the Azure service(s) and techniques you will use.
  2. Build: follow your proposal and deploy your prototype on Azure. Keep a weekly log of your progress.
  3. Document: describe your final architecture, estimate what it would cost, and explain the security choices you made.
  4. Log & deliver: hand in your logbook, a link to your live prototype, and a short summary of what works.
