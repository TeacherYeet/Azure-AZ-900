AZ-900 Exam Preparation

This section pulls together everything you have learned about cloud concepts and Azure, and gets you ready for the real Microsoft AZ-900: Azure Fundamentals exam. You will review the material domain by domain, drill the key terminology, and finish with a full timed mock exam.

💡 What AZ-900 actually tests
AZ-900 is Microsoft's entry-level certification for Azure. It measures your knowledge across three skill domains:
  • Describe cloud concepts — what cloud computing is, service and deployment models, and the benefits of the cloud.
  • Describe Azure architecture and services — Azure's core infrastructure, compute, networking, storage, and identity/security services.
  • Describe Azure management and governance — cost management, governance tools, and the tools used to deploy and monitor Azure resources.
Everything below is organized around these three domains.

How to use this section

Work through the pages in order. Each review lesson is a compact summary — not a full re-teach — so make sure you have already completed the lessons in Assignments 1–4 first. After the three domain reviews, use the flashcard deck and the mock exam to check whether you are ready.
