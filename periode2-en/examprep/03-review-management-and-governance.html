Review: Management and Governance

A compact reference covering how you keep control of cost, compliance, and operations once you have resources running in Azure. This maps to Microsoft's "Describe Azure management and governance" exam domain.

Cost management

ToolPurpose
Pricing CalculatorEstimates the cost of Azure products before you buy — configure region, tier, billing and support options. Gives estimates, not exact quotes.
Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) CalculatorEstimates the savings of moving an existing on-premises workload to Azure, by comparing on-premises costs against equivalent Azure costs.
Azure Cost ManagementReporting, budgets, alerts, and recommendations for controlling ongoing Azure spend.
TagsName-value metadata pairs attached to resources, used to organize resources and roll up billing information (e.g. by department or project).

Factors that affect Azure cost include: resource type, consumption (usage), maintenance, geography, network traffic (outbound data is billed; most inbound is free), and subscription type.

Governance and compliance

ToolPurpose
Azure PolicyEnforces organizational rules and assesses compliance at scale — evaluates resources and flags ones that don't comply (e.g. requiring a specific region or VM size).
Azure BlueprintsLets teams rapidly provision governed environments, bundling role assignments, policy assignments, ARM templates, and resource groups into one repeatable package.
Resource locksPrevent accidental deletion or modification of resources. CanNotDelete (Delete lock) allows reads/updates but blocks deletion; ReadOnly allows reads only.
Service Trust portalProvides Microsoft's compliance reports, audits, and information about how Microsoft secures and handles customer data.
💡 Exam tip
Azure Policy controls what configurations are allowed (compliance). Azure RBAC controls who can do what (access). They solve different problems and are often tested together.

Management and deployment tools

ToolPurpose
Azure Resource Manager (ARM)The management layer behind every way of interacting with Azure (Portal, CLI, PowerShell, SDKs). Lets you create, update, organize, and delete resources and resource groups consistently.
ARM templatesJSON files that declaratively define the infrastructure to deploy — repeatable, modular, and version-controllable "infrastructure as code."
Azure ArcExtends Azure management to resources outside Azure — on-premises servers, other clouds, and Kubernetes clusters — so they can be managed and governed like native Azure resources.

Monitoring tools

ToolPurpose
Azure AdvisorAnalyzes your deployed resources and gives personalized recommendations across reliability, security, performance, cost, and operational excellence.
Azure Service HealthThree related views: Azure Status (global health of all Azure services), Service Health (only the services/regions you use), and Resource Health (the health of your specific resources).
Azure MonitorCollects, analyzes, and acts on telemetry from cloud and on-premises environments — includes Application Insights, Log Analytics, alerts, and dashboards.
